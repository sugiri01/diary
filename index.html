TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
INITIAL

1.1 The Freelancer shall be engaged as part of the Frontal Designs team to provide design, branding, UI/UX services specifically for the Company's clients, including but not limited to Airvue and future clients.

1.2 The Freelancer agrees to provide the following services:

  • Logo design and brand identity development, including creation of primary and secondary logos, icon systems, and visual identity guidelines
  • UI/UX design for web and mobile applications, including wireframing, prototyping, user flows, and interaction design
  • Visual design assets as required by the Company, including marketing materials, social media graphics, presentations, and other collateral
  • Design system creation and maintenance, ensuring consistency across all client touchpoints
  • Conversion of design concepts into production-ready assets
  • Other design services as assigned by the Company

1.3 The Freelancer shall work under the direction of the Company and shall report directly to the Company's designated representative.

1.4 The Freelancer shall be available during standard business hours for communication and collaboration with the Company team. The parties may agree to specific hours of availability in writing.

1.5 The Freelancer shall adhere to all project deadlines and quality standards established by the Company. Failure to meet deadlines or quality standards may result in termination of the Agreement as provided herein.

2. TERM
INITIAL

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date of signing and shall continue until terminated in accordance with the provisions contained herein.

2.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party.

2.3 The Company reserves the right to terminate this Agreement immediately for cause, including but not limited to breach of confidentiality, non-compete, or other material provisions of this Agreement.

3. COMPENSATION
INITIAL

3.1 The Company shall compensate the Freelancer as follows: [Payment Terms]

3.2 Payment shall be made upon submission of invoices by the Freelancer and approval by the Company. Invoices should be submitted on the last day of each month and include detailed documentation of hours worked and tasks completed.

3.3 The Company will make payment within fourteen (14) business days of receiving and approving an invoice.

3.4 The Freelancer shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and other obligations related to the compensation received under this Agreement.

3.5 The Freelancer acknowledges that they are not entitled to overtime pay, holiday pay, vacation pay, sick leave, health insurance, retirement benefits, or any other employee benefits from the Company.

3.6 Expenses incurred by the Freelancer in the performance of services under this Agreement shall only be reimbursable if:

  • The expenses are reasonable and necessary for the performance of services
  • The expenses are pre-approved in writing by the Company
  • The Freelancer provides documentation (such as receipts) substantiating the expenses

3.7 If a project is cancelled or substantially modified after work has commenced, the Freelancer shall be entitled to compensation for work completed up to that point, based on the agreed-upon rate.

4. RELATIONSHIP OF PARTIES
INITIAL

4.1 The Freelancer is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee of the Company.

4.2 Nothing contained in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, or employer-employee relationship between the Parties.

4.3 The Freelancer shall not be entitled to any benefits, including but not limited to health insurance, paid leave, or retirement benefits that the Company may offer to its employees.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE
INITIAL

5.1 The Freelancer acknowledges that during the course of providing services, they may have access to confidential and proprietary information of the Company and its clients, including but not limited to business plans, marketing strategies, client lists, financial information, technical specifications, proprietary algorithms, design methodologies, trade secrets, and patented or proprietary technologies (collectively, "Confidential Information").

5.2 The Freelancer agrees to:

  • Maintain the strictest confidentiality regarding all Confidential Information
  • Not disclose, reproduce, or use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than performing services for the Company
  • Take all reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information, including but not limited to using password protection on devices storing Confidential Information
  • Not make any copies of Confidential Information except as necessary to perform services under this Agreement
  • Store Confidential Information securely and separately from the Freelancer's own information
  • Return all Confidential Information and related materials upon termination of this Agreement or upon request by the Company
  • Delete or destroy any Confidential Information stored electronically upon termination of this Agreement or upon request by the Company

5.3 The Freelancer specifically acknowledges that they may come across patented technologies and innovations while working on client projects, particularly with Airvue. The Freelancer shall not:

  • Reveal these technologies to competitors or any third party not authorized by the Company
  • Use or adapt these technologies to create their own products or services
  • Attempt to reverse engineer, decompile, or otherwise determine the functioning of these technologies
  • File any patent, trademark, or other intellectual property application related to these technologies
  • Advise others about these technologies or assist others in developing similar technologies

5.4 The Freelancer acknowledges that any breach of these confidentiality obligations may cause irreparable harm to the Company and/or its clients, for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief to prevent any breach or threatened breach of these obligations.

5.5 The obligations of confidentiality under this clause shall survive the termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

6. NON-COMPETE
INITIAL

6.1 During the term of this Agreement and for a period of two (2) years following its termination, the Freelancer agrees not to:

  • Engage in any business that competes directly with the Company
  • Provide similar services to any direct competitor of the Company
  • Develop or market products or services that would compete with the Company's offerings

6.2 The geographical scope of this non-compete provision shall extend to [Geographical Area].

6.3 The Freelancer acknowledges that this non-compete provision is reasonable and necessary to protect the legitimate business interests of the Company.

7. NON-SOLICITATION AND ANTI-POACHING
INITIAL

7.1 During the term of this Agreement and for a period of two (2) years following its termination, the Freelancer agrees not to:

  • Directly or indirectly solicit or attempt to solicit any client or customer of the Company for whom the Freelancer provided services
  • Directly or indirectly solicit or attempt to solicit any employee, contractor, or consultant of the Company to terminate their relationship with the Company

7.2 The Freelancer shall not, under any circumstances, collaborate directly with the Company's clients, including but not limited to Airvue, without the express written consent of the Company.

7.3 All client interactions must be conducted through or with the knowledge of the Company.

INITIAL

8.1 All work product, including but not limited to designs, logos, brand identities, user interfaces, user experiences, artwork, code, sketches, wireframes, prototypes, mockups, visual designs, style guides, and other deliverables created by the Freelancer in the course of providing services under this Agreement ("Work Product"), shall be the exclusive property of the Company.

8.2 The Freelancer hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest throughout the world in and to the Work Product, including:

  • All copyright rights and all rights under copyright
  • All patent rights and all rights to patent applications
  • All trademark rights and all rights to register trademarks
  • All trade secret rights
  • All moral rights and rights of attribution
  • All other intellectual and industrial property rights of every kind and nature and however designated, whether arising by operation of law, contract, license, or otherwise

8.3 The Freelancer shall execute any documents and take any actions necessary to perfect the Company's ownership of the Work Product, including completing and signing all documents confirming the Company's ownership of the Work Product, both during the term of this Agreement and thereafter.

8.4 The Freelancer shall not use any Work Product for their own purposes or for the benefit of any third party without the express written consent of the Company. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Using designs or concepts in the Freelancer's portfolio without permission
  • Repurposing design elements for other clients
  • Displaying or showcasing the Work Product on social media, personal websites, or any other platform

8.5 The Freelancer warrants that all Work Product will be original work and will not infringe upon the intellectual property rights of any third party. The Freelancer shall not incorporate any third-party intellectual property into the Work Product without the prior written consent of the Company.

8.7 The Freelancer agrees to waive any moral rights in the Work Product to the extent permitted by applicable law.

9. PROHIBITION ON SUBCONTRACTING
INITIAL

9.1 The Freelancer shall not subcontract or delegate any of the services to be provided under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the Company.

9.2 In the event that the Company permits the Freelancer to engage a subcontractor, the Freelancer shall remain fully responsible for the quality and timeliness of all services.

9.3 Any approved subcontractor must agree in writing to comply with all terms of this Agreement, including but not limited to confidentiality, non-compete, and intellectual property provisions.

10. REPRESENTATION AS TEAM MEMBER
INITIAL

10.1 The Freelancer agrees to represent themselves as part of the Frontal Designs team when interacting with clients or third parties in connection with services provided under this Agreement.

10.2 When attending client meetings, participating in calls, or communicating via email or other channels, the Freelancer shall:

  • Use a Frontal Designs email address provided by the Company (if applicable)
  • Identify themselves as a Designer/Developer/Consultant (as appropriate) with Frontal Designs
  • Maintain a professional appearance and demeanor consistent with the Company's standards
  • Not disclose their independent contractor status to clients unless explicitly authorized by the Company
  • Present work product as being created by "the Frontal Designs team" rather than attributing it solely to themselves

10.3 The Freelancer shall not market or promote themselves individually to the Company's clients. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Sharing personal contact information with clients
  • Soliciting direct work from clients
  • Directing clients to personal websites, portfolios, or social media profiles
  • Distributing personal business cards to clients
  • Discussing potential work outside the scope of the Company's engagement

10.4 All communications with clients shall be conducted in a manner consistent with the Company's brand, values, and communication guidelines. The Freelancer shall:

  • Use approved templates, letterheads, and presentation formats
  • Maintain the tone, style, and level of formality established by the Company
  • Adhere to any communication protocols or scripts provided by the Company
  • Copy appropriate Company personnel on client communications as directed

10.5 The Freelancer shall not make any representations, warranties, or commitments on behalf of the Company without prior written authorization.

10.6 The Freelancer shall not disparage or speak negatively about the Company, its personnel, or its practices to clients or any third parties.

13. QUALITY AND PERFORMANCE STANDARDS
INITIAL

13.1 The Freelancer agrees to perform all services in a professional manner consistent with industry standards and the Company's quality expectations.

13.2 All deliverables must meet the following criteria:

  • Adhere to any specifications, briefs, or guidelines provided by the Company
  • Be organized and structured in a clear, understandable manner
  • For design work, include appropriate file organization, layer naming, and component structure
  • Be delivered in the file formats specified by the Company
  • Be free from technical errors, spelling mistakes, and grammatical issues
  • Comply with any applicable industry standards or best practices

13.3 If a deliverable does not meet the Company's quality standards, the Freelancer shall promptly make necessary revisions at no additional cost to the Company.

13.4 The Company may provide feedback or request revisions to any deliverable. The Freelancer shall address such feedback or revision requests in a timely manner.

13.5 The Freelancer acknowledges that meeting deadlines is critical to the Company's business. Repeated failure to meet deadlines shall be grounds for termination of this Agreement.
